## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, analytical environment tailored for ambitious knowledge workers, founders, and teams obsessed with time sovereignty. It rejects sterile enterprise dashboard tropes in favor of an energizing, hyper-lucid aesthetic that treats personal time allocation as an actionable canvas rather than a ledger of guilt. 

The emotional tone balances calm analytical command with kinetic motivation:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** Dense telemetry without cognitive fatigue. Every micro-chart, badge, and metric exists to prompt proactive agency.
- **Vibrant Diagnostic Contrast:** Deep, muted base planes offset by luminous, chromatic focal points that immediately guide attention to temporal leaks and sustained focus blocks.
- **Architectural Refinement:** Rooted in Modern Precision with subtle frosted glass accents, ultra-fine boundary definition, and pillular geometry that softens dense analytical feeds into digestible modules.

## Colors

The system uses a dark-dominant canvas engineered to eliminate eye fatigue during all-day monitoring, leveraging luminous, semantic accents for rapid cognitive parsing.

### Core Canvas & Structural Neutral
- **Base Surface (`#090D16`):** Deep abyssal canvas behind root viewport panels.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0F172A`):** Core application frame, panels, and foundational layout shells.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Interactive card planes, timeline tracks, and input surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#334155`):** Hover states, active controls, and elevated overlays.
- **Boundary Definers (`#334155` at 40–60% opacity):** Sub-pixel border trims providing optical crispness.

### Semantic & Category Visualizations
- **Electric Violet (`#6366F1`):** Primary action catalyst, brand anchor, active AI interventions, and selected states.
- **Mint Emerald (`#10B981`):** Deep focus, uninterrupted flow blocks, goal attainment, and positive trends.
- **Soft Sky Blue (`#0EA5E9`):** Restorative breaks, physical recharge, reflection periods, and structural metadata.
- **Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Synchronous meetings, collaborative reviews, and context switches.
- **Coral Rose (`#F43F5E`):** Distractions, fragmentation warnings, burnout risk indicators, and urgent overrides.

### Text & Iconography Contrast Tiers
- **High-Emphasis Content (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary metrics, critical headlines, and focused timeline blocks.
- **Medium-Emphasis Content (`#94A3B8`):** Descriptions, supporting metrics, inactive labels, and axis markers.
- **Low-Emphasis Muted (`#64748B`):** Time stamps, empty track slots, disabled inputs, and decorative grid lines.

## Typography

The design system exclusively employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project a clean, high-density, yet accessible tech-forward aesthetic. 

- **Display & Numerical Clarity:** All numeric metric displays leverage tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time timeline recalculations and live ticker operations.
- **Typographic Scale:** Strict proportioning pairs tight negative tracking on headlines with elevated tracking on micro-labels to preserve structural hierarchy across dense visual distributions.
- **Hierarchy Distinction:** Section headers rely on weight (`700` and `800`) paired with negative letter spacing, whereas timeline categories and system tags use uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`) with wide spacing (`0.04em`) for instant scanning against tinted badge backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes an explicit 8-point base grid (subdivided into 4px increments for micro-alignments such as chip padding and status bullets).

### Layout Geometry
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fluid 12-column dynamic framework configured with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. The left utility console spans 3 columns, the central interactive timeline and heatmaps command 6 columns, and the contextual AI recommendation engine anchors the remaining 3 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column structure with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. The AI recommendation tray stacks beneath the central timeline stream as collapsible drawer modules.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column reflow framework utilizing `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Top navigation shifts to an anchor bottom dock; timelines switch from horizontal continuous ribbons to a vertical chronotrack view.

### Rhythmic Padding Standards
- **Component Tightness (`space-xs` & `space-sm`):** Reserved for chip internal padding, icon-to-label inline offsets, and dense sub-track hourly markers.
- **Container Gaps (`space-md` & `space-lg`):** Standard structural breathing room within dashboard cards, analytical widgets, and grouped form elements.
- **Section Separation (`space-xl`):** Canvas demarcations between distinct analytical modules (e.g., Timeline Canvas vs. Heatmap Distribution Matrix).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual plane differentiation avoids conventional high-opacity drop shadows. Instead, it relies on a layered combination of translucent surface tiers, low-contrast ghost borders, and contextual luminous glows:

- **Surface 0 (Floor):** Pure `#090D16`, matte, absorbing light.
- **Surface 1 (Base Panels & Shell):** Deep slate `#0F172A` with a 1px perimeter outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Surface 2 (Floating Cards & Active Blocks):** Backed by `#1E293B` at 80% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlines use a directional top-edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and bottom-edge shadow (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Elevation Layer 3 (Modals, Hover Badges, Tooltips):** Background `#1E293B` elevated with a tinted ambient glow rather than neutral black shadow:
  - Default: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 0 1px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`
  - Active Productivity States: Diffused mint emission `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`
  - Alert/Burnout Warning States: Diffused coral emission `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a distinct Pill-Shaped architectural language (`level 3`). This high-curvature system creates a warm, ergonomic contrast against dense, mathematical data displays.

- **Micro Radii (Pills & Badges):** All interactive action triggers, category markers, and time indicators use complete circular caps (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Standard Containers (`rounded-lg` / `2rem`):** Analytical dashboard modules, individual focus summary pods, and heatmap sections utilize sweeping 32px radii, establishing sleek, floating containment.
- **Macro Overlays (`rounded-xl` / `3rem`):** Slide-over AI suggestion drawers, deep-focus full-screen modal overlays, and quick-action bottom sheets on mobile devices feature bold 48px contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (AI/Apply):** Pill capsule (`rounded-full`), solid `#6366F1`, text `#F8FAFC` (`label-lg`), subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`). Hover expands a 12px soft indigo glow.
- **Secondary (Neutral Shift):** Surface `#1E293B` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#94A3B8`. Hover transitions text to `#F8FAFC` and surface to `#334155`.
- **Tertiary Ghost:** No border or background; text `#94A3B8`. Hover yields text `#6366F1` and background `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Metric Badges
- **Structure:** Pill container (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 12px`, featuring an integrated `6px` circular status beacon.
- **Semantic Variants:**
  - *Deep Focus:* Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, beacon solid `#10B981`.
  - *Meetings:* Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#F59E0B`, beacon solid `#F59E0B`.
  - *Distraction Risk:* Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`, text `#F43F5E`, beacon pulsing `#F43F5E`.
  - *Recharge:* Background `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.12)`, text `#0EA5E9`, beacon solid `#0EA5E9`.

### Timeline Chronotrack Blocks
- Rounded segment capsules (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl` depending on duration length) with translucent fills (20% opacity) and intense 1.5px solid boundary edges matching category token colors.
- Micro-interactions: Hover triggers scale (`1.02`), brings the block forward in z-index, and activates an elevated contextual metric card.

### Cards
- **Base Container:** Background `#0F172A` paired with `rounded-lg` (32px), bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Internal padding defaults to `space-lg`.
- **AI Recommendation Card:** Distinct frosted glass background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle gradient border from `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)` to `transparent`. Features a prominent primary pill button for direct automation approval.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Pill shaped (`rounded-full`), height `44px`, background `#1E293B`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`. Focus transitions the border to solid `#6366F1` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular (`rounded-full`), `20px` diameter, background `#1E293B`, boundary `1.5px solid #64748B`. Checked state fills solid `#6366F1` containing a crisp white tick or core dot.

### Categorized Distribution Heatmap
- Dense geometric array of micro-rounded capsules (`rounded-sm` / 4px) mapping daily hourly intervals. 
- Opacity gradient reflects focus intensity (e.g., emerald variants transitioning from 15% saturation up to 100% luminous intensity for multi-hour uninterrupted flows).
